# Cinematic Opening Storyboard

| Scene | Camera Position | Camera Movement | Venue Feature | Text | Approx. Scroll Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 01 — Establishing | Wide, slightly elevated exterior | Hold, then very slow forward dolly | Full architecture | “Where extraordinary celebrations begin.” | 0–23% |
| 02 — Approach | Lower and more aligned with entrance | Forward dolly + tiny lateral correction | Main entrance | “A landmark arrival.” | 23–42% |
| 03 — Threshold | Eye-level through entrance axis | Stronger forward move, brightness lift | Entrance / lobby | “Crossing the threshold.” | 42–62% |
| 04 — Grand Hall | Wide rear/central hall perspective | Reveal, settle, deliberate hold | Main hall scale | “The grand reveal.” | 62–83% |
| 05 — Stage Focus | Centreline, slightly lower | Gentle push toward stage | Stage / ceremony area | “Built around the moment.” | 83–100% |

## Prototype notes

The prototype uses photography and layered transforms to demonstrate the desired camera rhythm without pretending to be an accurate 3D model. The real production implementation can replace each scene with a GLB/GLTF camera spline, a depth-mapped 2.5D scene, cinematic video, or a hybrid of all three.
